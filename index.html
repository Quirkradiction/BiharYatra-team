The Tourism Dilemma & Our Fix

Why BiharYatra Exists

A tourism-rich state still needs easier, personalized trip planning for everyday travelers.

Scattered Information

Destination details, public transit routes, approximate costs, and guides require checking fragmented, outdated sources.

One-Size-Fits-All

A static checklist of tourist spots fails to answer: "What can I experience practically within my exact 3 days and ₹3,000 budget?"

Popular Places Dominate

Crowds flock to common spots while stunning rural handicraft clusters, ecotourism, and ruins stay under-explored.
